# Creative Agency Financial Dashboard: Simulated Case Study

## Project Overview

This project is a simulated financial analysis and management-reporting case study for a fictional creative agency.

The workbook analyzes the agency's revenue, operating expenses, budget performance, profitability, monthly trends, and client concentration. The goal was to move beyond transaction-level processing and use financial data to evaluate overall business performance.

The completed dashboard provides a summarized view of financial results while supporting the underlying figures with detailed monthly, budget, and client-level analysis.

## Business Problem

Creative agencies often manage revenue across multiple clients while balancing payroll, contractors, software, marketing, and other operating expenses.

Reviewing revenue alone does not provide enough information to understand the financial health of the business. Management also needs visibility into profitability, budget performance, monthly trends, expense behavior, and dependence on individual clients.

The goal of this project was to organize financial data into a reporting system that could answer those questions and make important performance trends easier to identify.

## Project Results

The completed analysis produced the following financial results:

- **$205,900** in total revenue
- **$100,170** in total operating expenses
- **$105,730** in operating profit
- **51.35%** operating margin
- **$4,900 favorable revenue variance** compared with budget
- **$4,470 favorable expense variance** compared with budget

The analysis identified **Foxglove Tech** as the agency's highest-revenue client, representing approximately **20.74% of total revenue**.

Monthly profitability analysis identified **June** as the strongest operating-profit month and **January** as the lowest operating-profit month in the reporting period.

Client-level revenue calculations were also reconciled to total reported revenue, providing an additional control over the dashboard's summarized financial results.

## Key Financial Insights

The agency generated an operating profit of **$105,730** on **$205,900** of revenue, resulting in an operating margin of approximately **51.35%**.

Actual revenue exceeded budget by **$4,900**, while operating expenses were **$4,470 below budget**. Together, these results contributed to stronger-than-budgeted operating performance.

Client concentration analysis showed that the largest client represented approximately **20.74% of total revenue**, providing visibility into how much of the agency's revenue depended on a single client relationship.

Monthly analysis also showed variation in profitability throughout the reporting period, with June producing the highest operating profit and January producing the lowest.
